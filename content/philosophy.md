# Our philosophy

**Why we do this**
Mansel Live exists to build a second source of income from money we don't need. It isn't our savings, emergency fund or retirement money; that stays in plain index funds and stays boring. This is only surplus money, a fixed amount we can afford to see go down without it affecting our life.

**What success looks like**
Steady, modest returns over years, not quick wins. We judge ourselves against one simple benchmark: the Nifty 50. If we can't beat just holding the index after costs and tax, we should hold the index instead, and we will.

**How much risk we take**
- At most about a 15% fall from peak before we stop and rethink.
- No borrowing, no leverage, no futures or options.
- We add money only from genuine surplus, never to "win back" a loss.

**How a strategy earns real money**
1. It's written as clear rules, with no gut feel.
2. It's tested on old data with real costs, taxes and realistic fills.
3. It's checked once on data it has never seen. We don't tweak the rules after looking at results.
4. Most ideas fail this. That's normal, and it's money we didn't lose.
5. A strategy that comes close but doesn't pass everything can run only as a small, clearly labelled pilot, with a stop rule decided in advance.

**How the system runs**
- It trades by its rules, not by news, tips or mood.
- Every position has a defined way out.
- One person logs in to Kite each morning. The system does the rest, and every action is recorded.

**What we don't do**
Tips, chasing hot stocks, trading on the news, overriding the system mid-trade, or adding money to "average down" a losing idea.

**When we stop or change course**
- The book falls past its agreed limit.
- Real results drift well away from what testing promised, after a fair amount of time (at least 3 months).
- A rule turns out to be broken. Then we fix the rule first, and only then trade again.

**How we review**
- Weekly: what was bought and sold, and why.
- Monthly: our results against the Nifty 50.
- Quarterly: keep, resize or stop each strategy.

**Honest expectations**
Some months, even some years, will lose money. Returns will be modest. The point is to grow surplus money with discipline, and to learn without risking what matters.
